🔍 Key Research Areas

  • What problem does the project solve?
  • What technology does it use?
  • Who develops or maintains it?
  • What are its main features?
  • Where can its documentation be verified?

✅ Verification Checklist

  • Check official documentation.
  • Compare project claims with independent information.
  • Review development activity.
  • Identify important limitations and risks.

❓ Open Questions

  • What information is still missing?
  • Which claims require additional verification?
  • What assumptions should be tested?
